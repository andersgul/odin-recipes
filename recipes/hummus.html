Hummus recipe

Take me back to the home page for more recipes.

Hummus us a versatile dish that can be used in everything from tacos to your slice of bread. A favorite in the Arab world, this will be a true companion for you.

This simple recipe tastes fantastic, and you will make over and over again.

Ingredients

This recipe give 8 portions

  • 400 gr boiled chick peas
  • 0.5 ts cumin
  • 2 sp tahini
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 2 sp lemon juice
  • 2 sp olive oil
  • Salt

Step by step

  • Rinse the chick peas well
  • Run the peas through a mixer
  • Add the rest of the ingredients and mix until smoothj

If the hummus is too thick, you can add a little bit of water when mixing.
