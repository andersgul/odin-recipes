Pizza is a loved dish all across the world. It can be made simple, and you can learn how to make a good pizza in no time. On the other hand, people spend their entire life chasing the perfect pizza. How you want to do it is up to you.

This is simple, but super nice recipe. It can be made quickly, and is the perfect Friday pizza recipe.

Ingredients

This recipe give 8 portions

  • 6 dl water
  • 1 dl olive oil
  • 3 gr yeast
  • 1 ts sugar
  • 1 kg flour - tipo 00 is preferred
  • Salt

Step by step

  • Mix water, olive oil, yeast and sugar for about 3 minutes
  • Add flour and salt. Knead the dough for about 15 minutes. The dough should be smooth and strong.
  • Put the dough on a table with flour on it, let it sit for 20 minutes
  • Devide the dough into 8 pieces, and let it rise for 2-3 hours

After the dough has rised, make pizza. Add your favorite topping.
