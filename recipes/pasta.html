Pasta! Do we need to say anyhting more? Homemade pasta will make you life better.

This pasta recipe will take your italian dishes to new places. Simple, easy and incredible good.

Ingredients

This recipe give 4 portions

  • 400 gr flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 sp olive oil
  • 0.5 ts salt

Step by step

  • Have all the flour in a high pile on the table. Carve out a whole in the middle of the flour pile.
  • Crack all the eggs into the whole in the flour.
  • Work the flour into the eggs with a fork. Work in circles, and continue until everything is mixed into a dough.
  • Knead the dough for 15 minutes, until it gets elastic.
  • Wrap the dough in plastic, and leave it in the fridge for 20 minutes

When you are ready, run the dough through a pasta maker.
